Melee Shield-Tilt Explorer

Where each character's shield bubble sits for any stick angle and strength, with the hurtboxes in that tilted pose, and how much of the body is left outside the shield (open to shield pokes). Side view in game units, facing right (x = forward, y = up).

Computed from vanilla NTSC 1.02 game data and the decompiled engine logic. Every character's bubble position was checked against Dolphin, with a maximum error under 0.00001 units. Nana is identical to Popo.

Hurtboxes are coloured from blue (fully inside the bubble) to orange (fully outside). Orange dots mark the parts outside the bubble, seen from the side; because they are measured in 3D, a dot can appear over the bubble when that part sticks out towards or away from the camera. Sampled every 5° and every 0.2 of stick strength. Kirby's and Jigglypuff's bubbles are slightly oval in game; here they are drawn as spheres of the average radius. Hurtboxes marked ≈ sit on physics-driven bones and use the rest pose, so they are approximate. Yoshi's shield neither tilts nor changes size.
